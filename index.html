// Highlights

Key Features

The Shrinking Zone

The arena tightens stage by stage on a schedule the host sets. Hiding spots expire, distances close, and no round ever ends in a stalemate.

Nine Game Modes

Classic Hide & Seek, Zombie Infection, high-speed Chase, Assassin, Freeze Tag, Mr. X Hunt, Sardines, Murder Mystery and Meteor Escape. One map, nine very different game nights.

Tactical Items

Scan for hiders, plant Proximity Alarms, move the zone, or activate Ghost Mode as Seekers. Deploy Decoys, Zone Shields, and Camouflage to survive as Hiders.

Real-Time GPS

Live positions, zone checks and tag range all run on real GPS. Your movement is the controller. No buttons, just instinct.

Instant Private Lobbies

Share a short Zone ID and your crew is in. From three friends to 20+ players, with no accounts and no sign-ups.

Privacy First

No email sign-up required. Location, player names and chat are used for live lobby gameplay, with automated cleanup for finished or abandoned matches.
